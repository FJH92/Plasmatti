DEMO DATA - INVENTED, NOT REAL MEASUREMENTS

The solid-sample demo. The measurements are the same invented ones as in the liquid demo; here they are
treated as digests of solid samples (0.25 to 0.5 g, digested and made up to 50 ml). No value comes from a
real sample or laboratory.

The folder holds what a real solid session holds:
- the measurement file ("replicates as csv"), in the PerkinElmer Optima layout;
- the autosampler file, which gives the sample names (the digestion blanks are "Blank 1" and "Blank 2");
- the dissolution file, written as the laboratory writes it: a sample column and "Weight (g)", the final
  volume once above the table ("to 50 ml"), and "xxx" for the blank, which has no mass.

What to look for:
- Step 1: the dissolution file is found next to the measurements.
- Step 3: the masses and the volume are filled in from the dissolution file, each with its source
  ("dissolution file, row N"); "Fish meal 1:10" and "Seaweed 1:10" find their masses under "Fish meal" and
  "Seaweed", and the dilution 10 is taken from the name.
- Results: mg/kg of the sample (result = C x volume / mass x dilution) next to the mg/L of the digest; the limit
  of each sample is LOQ x volume / mass x dilution, so Garden soil and Lake sediment (0.25 g) have twice the
  limit of the 0.5 g samples.
